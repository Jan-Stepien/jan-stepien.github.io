G.O.A.T Run

The farm's fastest goat is on the loose. Dash, jump and roll through a bright 3D world that bends and rolls under your hooves, from the farm to a small town, a busy city and out to Mars.

  • One-thumb controls, easy to learn and hard to put down
  • Ramps, tractors and tin cans, new worlds as you go
  • Daily challenge, quests and a leaderboard
  • Free to play on iPhone and Android
