Goat Run privacy policy

Last updated: 6 October 2026

Goat Run is a game by Jan Stepien (Legit software solutions). This page explains what the game does with information. The short version: there is no account, we do not collect your name or email, and the game does not track you across other companies' apps unless you allow it.

What stays on your device

Your high score, Tin Cans, boosts, quests, achievements, settings and daily-reward progress are stored on your device only. We do not receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, approximate location (from your IP address), and information about how you interact with ads, as described in Google's advertising policy.

  • On iPhone and iPad the game asks, using Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether it may use your advertising identifier. If you say no, you still see ads, but they are not personalised and your identifier is not used.
  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and some other regions, the game shows Google's consent form first, and ads follow your choice. You can change your choice later in the game's privacy options where shown, or in your device settings.
  • You can switch the between-run ads off for good with the one-time Remove Ads purchase. Optional rewarded ads stay available because you choose to watch them.

Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Purchases are made through the App Store; Apple handles your payment and we never see your payment details. To confirm purchases and give you what you bought, the game uses RevenueCat, which receives a random anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app and basic device information. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not do

  • We do not ask you to create an account.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, microphone or precise location.
  • We do not sell your information.

Children

Goat Run is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent and think your child has given us information, contact us and we will help.

Your choices and rights

You can reset your advertising identifier or turn off tracking in your device's Settings → Privacy. Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information a service holds about you, to correct it or to have it deleted. Because we hold no personal information ourselves, requests about advertising data are best sent to Google, and requests about purchase data to RevenueCat; you can also write to us and we will point you the right way.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.
